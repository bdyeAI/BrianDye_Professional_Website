My vision for statewide data collections

Accurate statewide reporting begins with clear definitions, reliable checks, and school division staff who know what is expected. I would focus on helping divisions understand requirements early, identify issues before submission deadlines, and resolve problems with consistent, useful guidance.

Good processes should support accuracy without creating unnecessary work. I believe in listening to the people completing collections and using that feedback to improve documentation, training, and tools.

Leadership approach

I believe effective leadership starts with listening, following through, and making complicated work easier for others. I try to explain the reason behind a process, provide practical examples, and build confidence rather than simply handing people instructions.

My years as a teacher still shape how I train adults and support school divisions.
